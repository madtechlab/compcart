🟢 1. First Time Setup ▼
1
Go to the Settings tab ⚙️
Tap Settings at the top of the screen
2
Add your stores
Under Stores, enter the store name (e.g. Walmart, Aldi) and an optional location, then tap + Add store
3
Repeat for all your stores
Do this once for every store you shop at — you only need to do it one time
✅ Done! Now you're ready to start tracking prices.
🏷️ 2. Adding Prices ▼

Three ways to add prices — pick whichever fits the moment:

Option A — Type it in manually
1
Go to the Prices tab
2
Fill in item name, category, store, and price
3
Tap + Add price entry
Option B — Scan a barcode 📷
1
Go to Import → tap Scan Barcode
2
Tap Start Camera Scanner and point at the barcode
3
Enter the price from the shelf, tap + Add to prices
💡 Best for in-store use — fastest workflow when you're shopping
Option C — Photo or receipt 🧾
1
Go to Import → tap Photo / Receipt
2
Take or select a photo of a price tag or receipt
3
Review the detected items and tap Add selected items
🤖 AI-powered — the app reads prices automatically from photos
🔍 3. Browsing Your Prices ▼

In the Prices tab you can search, sort, and filter your entries:

🔍 Search by name A–Z sort Price sort Filter by category ⏱ Filter by age
What the labels mean:
🏆 Best — lowest price for this item ⚠️ Stale — price over 60 days old ↑ Trend — price going up ↓ Trend — price going down
💡 Unit pricing: Tap the $/unit button to compare by ounce or pound instead of total price — great for different size packages
🛒 4. Building Your Shopping List ▼
1
Go to the Shop tab 🛒
Type an item name and tap Add — or tap the cart icon on any price entry
2
The app does the work ✨
Automatically finds the cheapest store for each item and groups your list by store
3
Shop and check off items
Tap the checkbox next to each item as you add it to your cart
What you'll see
🏪 Store grouped by best price$12.45
💰 Savings banner if splitting helpsSave $3.20
📋 Items with no price data—
💡 Use the Min. saving worth a separate trip slider to control when the app recommends splitting your shopping between stores
⭐ 5. Best Deals Tab ▼

Go to the Deals tab to see your complete savings picture:

✓
Cheapest store for every tracked item
✓
How much you save per item vs the highest price
✓
Total possible savings across all items
✓
Best Cart Cost — the absolute cheapest way to buy everything by cherry-picking the best price at each store
📊 6. Price History ▼

Every time you update a price the old one is saved automatically.

1
Tap the chart icon 〰️ on any price entry
Only appears after you've updated a price at least once
2
You'll see a price chart with:
High / Low / Average price, trend over time, and a 30d / 90d / 1yr / All range selector
📉 Price drop alert: If the current price is significantly below recent average, the chart shows a green banner — great for spotting when to stock up!
⚙️ 7. Helpful Settings ▼
📦
Export data

🔄
Import backup
Restore your data from a previously exported JSON file
🧹
Find & merge duplicates
If you've entered the same item with slightly different names, this tool finds and merges them into one
📵 8. Offline Mode ▼

No internet? No problem.

✓
The app still works fully without a connection
✓
Changes are saved locally to your device
✓
Syncs automatically the moment you reconnect
📵 A purple banner appears at the top when you're offline so you always know your connection status
💡 Tips for Best Results ▼
✔
Use consistent names
"Milk 1 gallon" instead of random variations — keeps comparisons accurate
✔
Include size when possible
"Peanut Butter 28 oz" — enables unit price comparison between different sizes
✔
Update prices regularly
Prices older than 60 days get a ⚠️ stale warning — keeps recommendations accurate
✔
Use barcode scanning in-store
Fastest workflow — scan, enter price, done in seconds
